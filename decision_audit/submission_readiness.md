## Positioning supported by current evidence

The strongest contribution is controlled measurement of how assigned trace length changes economic action quality, with careful scoring and uncertainty. The calculator audit strengthens the practical boundary of that contribution: improvement within the LLM pipeline does not establish superiority over numerical optimization. The contest contributes conditional allocation consequences of the chosen quality and selection assumptions. Three-region existence is structural; the manuscript identifies what the measured frontiers and economic weighting actually change.

The prepared human pilot could test choices among displayed benchmark profiles. It cannot, by itself, justify claims about actual service demand, willingness to pay or deployed market welfare. A journal/conference introduction should retain this distinction rather than treating a proposed study as completed evidence.
