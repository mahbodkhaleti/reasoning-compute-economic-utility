Comparing decision services

This pilot asks how you compare anonymous automated bidding services. The profiles summarize preserved results on 216 constructed auction decisions. The survey is hypothetical; no service is purchased and no auction is entered.

A higher “profit captured” score means more of the best feasible expected bidder profit was captured, averaged equally across tasks. It is not a probability of winning. Exact optimality counts tasks where the best specified bid was selected. Mean expected profit gives each dollar equal weight. These statistics can rank services differently.

Choose the service you would prefer for similar tasks at the displayed hypothetical fee. Model names, token budgets and service type are hidden. There is no required correct preference.

Only the code, choices, displayed profiles, presentation order and response durations are recorded. Your responses stay in this browser until you export a file and share it with the researcher. Use a code rather than your name. You may stop before export. A code beginning TEST marks a diagnostic session excluded from observed-response analysis.
