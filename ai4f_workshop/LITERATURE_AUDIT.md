The original survey, Shadow Price of Reasoning, Test-Time Compute Games, Bazaar, AucArena, Inference Scaling Laws and the ACL selective-QA paper were also checked against their primary landing pages. The OpenReview page for the synthetic-laboratory workshop paper served a browser-verification challenge; its metadata is retained rather than reconstructed from a secondary source. This partial external-access limit is not hidden by successful BibTeX compilation.

The distinguishing proposal is to prioritize extraction ambiguities by bounded consequences for action utility, with a conditional construction-regret stop. This provides an operational comparison, not evidence that no earlier robust optimization or value-of-information method uses similar ideas. No novelty-priority assertion is made. The historical supplied-probability benchmark does not empirically support the new repair policy.
