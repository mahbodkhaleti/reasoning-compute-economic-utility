# AI4F review with current editorial release

## Judgment

Subjective workshop-quality rating: **6.5/10**, borderline to weak accept. Topic fit is approximately **7.5/10**. These are reviewer judgments, not a formal venue rubric, measured scientific quantities or acceptance probabilities. The modest increase from v3's 6/10 reflects clearer positioning, operational admission and auditable CPU execution; it does not reflect newly demonstrated economic gains.

The manuscript is strongest as an evaluation paper with a prospective extension. The old calculator comparator, held-out utility, aggregation/validity analysis and resource boundaries remain the empirical contribution. The new source-interval policy is conditional and unevaluated on independent model/service cases. Its mathematical lemma is useful but straightforward, and is not a strong novelty claim by itself.
