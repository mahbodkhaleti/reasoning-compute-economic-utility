# Independent service evaluation still required

This is a protocol specification, not a completed or fully frozen experimental registration. No task count, timing estimate, confidence threshold or abstention payoff is inferred from held-out outcomes.

## Research question

Does decision-sensitive repair offer a better joint cost–held-out-profit–completion tradeoff than random repair or uniform extraction effort under matched output/call caps? A conditional certificate alone is not the outcome of this comparison.

## Fix before evaluating

- Independent source families and a public inclusion/unit contract. Separate development and timing-pilot families from evaluation families; source IDs and their transformations cannot cross splits. Reused diagnostic families are excluded from confirmatory evaluation.
- The raw-record source schema, any language adapter, source-price verification/admission rules and the field-ambiguity protocol. Validate simultaneous price containment and the correctness of the semantic source contract independently of the policy's own decisions.
- One checkpoint/revision, prompts, decoding defaults, all output caps, initial versus repair call allocation, total caps, per-chunk allowances, fixed chunk partition and tie rules.
- Construction-regret tolerance and offer/abstention policy. If bidder-profit comparisons depend on an abstention payoff, specify its institutional meaning and value before observing evaluation outcomes; no historical outputs are retroactively assigned such a payoff.
- Positive pilot cost estimates and evaluation sample size. Use target-server timing to cap the planned work; choose sample size before test outcomes rather than stopping on a favorable effect. A one-day bound is a scheduling target until measured.
- Primary paired family-level contrast(s), any noninferiority margin, uncertainty resampling, and multiplicity adjustment for additional confirmatory contrasts. Report exploratory analyses as exploratory.

## Matched comparators

Cheap extraction plus deterministic calculator; uniform higher-budget extraction plus calculator; selective repair; random-chunk repair with the same caps; public-text deterministic parser plus calculator; confidence-priority ablation. Include parser successes and failures. Verified-record calculation is an information-advantaged ceiling diagnostic, not a deployable fair comparator.

The strict CPU source grammar is deliberately parser-readable. Evaluating only that grammar cannot establish an LLM extraction advantage. Broader language requires a justified new admission profile and independent containment evidence; rewriting a reused source into the grammar does not supply confirmation.

## Required outputs

For every offered task, preserve source IDs/hashes, stage calls, configured caps, actual or explicitly unknown token counts, stage-specific time, rejected proposals, retained intervals, chosen bid, certificate, completion/abstention status and held-out scoring. Include failed calls and verification; avoid summing overlapping callback-wall and generation timers. Charge shared extraction to every compared workflow that requires it.

Report offered-decision profit under the predeclared abstention policy, conditional completed-decision profit, regret, coverage, extraction correctness, simultaneous containment failure and stage resources. Use matched family-level contrasts, report uncertainty, and show cost/profit/coverage jointly. A cheap policy with lower coverage cannot be called better on cost alone. The construction certificate does not bound held-out regret.

No automatic scale-up or priority/superiority claim follows from engineering tests, reused development traces or conditional algebra checks.
