## Understanding First-Price Auctions

A first-price auction is a type of ascending or descending auction where bidders submit a single bid, and the highest bidder wins the item. The critical distinction from a second-price auction (also known as a Vickrey auction) is that in a first-price auction, the winner pays their bid amount.
